A routine inspection during a tenancy catches problems while they are small and cheap to fix: a slow leak, early damp, a failing extractor fan. It’s also a chance to check safety alarms. Agree a time with the tenant first and respect their home.

Throughout the property

  • Damp and mould: window frames, ceiling corners, behind furniture, bathrooms.
  • Leaks: under sinks, around baths and showers, below radiators, ceiling stains under bathrooms.
  • Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms: test each one.
  • Windows and doors: do they open, close and lock?
  • Heating and hot water: any faults reported?

Kitchen

  • Extractor fan working and not clogged.
  • Sealant around the sink and worktops intact.
  • Appliances working; fridge door seals sound.

Bathrooms

  • Grout and sealant: cracks or black mould.
  • Extractor fan working.
  • Toilet base and taps: any drips.

Outside

  • Gutters and drains clear; fences and gates secure.
  • Bins and the garden as agreed in the tenancy.

Record it the same way every time

Use the same rooms and checklist as the check-in, so each inspection can be compared with the last. In DIY Inventory, choose Interim Inspection and start from the previous report: every item shows how it was last time, and the report lists what has changed.
