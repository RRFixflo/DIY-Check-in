Privacy

Your privacy

What DIY Inventory keeps, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

While you’re doing your inventory

Your inventory, including its photos, is saved on your own phone as you go, so you don’t lose your work. Nothing is stored by us until you finish and sign the report. Starting again deletes it from your phone.

{{AI_NOTE}}

When you finish

When you sign and finish, a copy of your report is sent to us and stored securely, so it can’t be lost. It includes what you entered: the property address, your name, your signature, the rooms, items and their condition, meter readings and keys, and your photos. Full-size copies of the photos are stored too, so the links in your report open them.

  • It’s also shared with the letting agent, so everyone has the same record.
  • Anyone with your report can open the photos in it from their links. Each report’s links use a long random key that can’t be guessed.

Photos

The app uses your camera only while you’re taking photos for your inventory. The date and time are added to each photo. We don’t use your location.

Messages

If you use the contact form, we keep your name, email, phone number (if given) and message so we can reply.

Cookies

We use one cookie to remember your access link or code on your phone, so you don’t need it again. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted where we don’t need to keep it. Contact us to ask.
