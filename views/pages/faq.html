Getting started

Do I need to download an app?

No. It runs in your phone’s web browser (Safari or Chrome). Open your link and start. You can add it to your home screen if you like.

I’ve paid. How do I unlock my report?

Open the link in your payment email on the phone your report is on. Or copy the link and paste it into the box on the finished screen (handy if your email opens links in a different browser). Your report unlocks straight away.

I’ve been given an access code. Where do I put it?

Type it into the box on the finished screen when your report is done, instead of paying. You can also enter it first, in the “Have an access code?” box on the pricing page.

Can I use a tablet or computer?

A phone works best, because you walk round with it and use its camera. A tablet with a camera works too.

Doing the inventory

Is it really AI-powered?

Yes. After each photo, our AI suggests the item’s condition, a short description, any marks or damage, and how clean it is. It’s a suggestion: you can always change it, and you sign the report as your own record.

How long does it take?

Most flats take under an hour. Bigger houses take longer. The app shows you exactly what’s left in each room.

How many photos do I need?

At least 8 in every room, including at least one of each item on the room’s checklist. You can take up to 25 per room.

What if something on the checklist isn’t there?

Mark it as not applicable. You can also add items the checklist doesn’t have.

When do I have to do a check-in?

A check-in report has to be started within 7 days of your move-in date. The sooner the better: do it before you unpack if you can.

What if I lose signal or close the page?

Your progress is saved on your phone as you go. Open the app again and carry on where you left off.

Can I use it for a check-out?

Yes. Choose the type when you start (check-in, check-out or inspection), and the report is titled to match.

Landlords and agents

Can I use it on more than one property?

Yes. Use it on as many properties as you like. Each report is paid for separately.

Do my tenants need to download an app?

No. They open a secure link in their phone’s browser to check the report and sign it.

How does the tenant sign the report?

When you finish, tap Send the sign-off link and send it by text, WhatsApp or email. The tenant looks through each room and its photos, can comment on any item, and signs with their finger. A signed page is added to the report, and you can see on the finished screen when it’s been signed.

How do I compare the check-out with the check-in?

Start the check-out from the check-in report: on the finished screen tap Start a check-out or inspection, choose the property from your list, or open the link on the report’s contents page. The rooms and checklists are copied, and the new report shows what has changed, the meter readings then and now, and photos side by side.

Can I do inspections during the tenancy?

Yes. Choose Interim Inspection or Renewal Inspection. Start it from the last report to see what has changed.

Your report

What happens when I finish?

Once you’ve signed and confirmed the declaration, your PDF report is made on your phone and sent off automatically. You can save it, open it or share it straight away.

Will my landlord or agent accept it?

The report records the condition of every room with photos that carry the date and time in the image itself, plus your signature and declaration. Send a copy to your landlord or agent at the start of the tenancy so everyone has the same record.

Can people see the photos full size?

Yes. The photos in the PDF are kept small so the file is easy to send. Tap any photo, or its link in the index at the back, to open it full size.

Can I change the report after I’ve finished?

No. Once it’s signed, the report is final. That’s what makes it a reliable record.

Does this replace professional advice?

No. DIY Inventory creates dated records of a property’s condition. It is not legal advice. For a dispute or a specific question, speak to a qualified professional.

Payment

How much does it cost?

It’s free to start. You pay £30 + VAT (£36) for each finished report you download: a check-in, a check-out or an inspection. See pricing.

When do I pay?

When your report is finished and signed. The app shows a Pay £30 + VAT button. After paying, open the link in your payment email on the same phone, or paste it into the app, and your report unlocks to download and send. It waits safely on your phone until then.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You only pay for each report you make.

Is a link good for more than one report?

Each paid link is for one report. You can still open it afterwards to see and send that report.
