Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Anything else? See all questions or send us a message.

How does the AI work?

After each photo, our AI looks at the item and suggests its condition (new, good, fair or poor), a short description, any marks or damage, and how clean it is. It’s a suggestion: you can change anything, and you sign the report as your own record.

Do I pay before I start?

No. It’s free to start and do your report. You pay £30 + VAT when it’s finished, to download it and send it.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay £30 + VAT for each report: a check-in, a check-out or an inspection.

Do my tenants need to download an app?

No. They open a secure link in their phone’s browser to check and sign the report.

Can I use it on more than one property?

Yes. Use it on as many properties as you like. Each report is paid for separately.

Can I use the report in a deposit dispute?

Yes, that’s what it’s built for. Deposit schemes such as the TDS, DPS and mydeposits decide disputes on evidence, above all how the property was at check-in compared with check-out. Your report gives them exactly that: every room photographed with the date and time burned into each photo, the condition of every item, meter readings and keys, signed by you and the tenant, and a check-out that sets out what has changed, side by side.
