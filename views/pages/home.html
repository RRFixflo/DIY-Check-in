Features

Everything you need, in one tool

Built for landlords, small agencies and tenants who want clear records without paying clerk fees on every visit.

Room-by-room checklists

Every room gets a checklist of what to photograph. Start the next visit from the last report and the same checklist comes with it.

Dated photos

The date and time are burned into every photo the moment it’s taken, and each photo is tied to its room and item.

Tenant sign-off

Send the tenant a secure link. They check each room on their phone, comment on anything, and sign. No app to download.

Side-by-side comparison

A check-out or inspection compares itself with the earlier report: what changed, meter readings then and now, and photos side by side.

Inspections, not just move-in

Mid-tenancy and renewal inspections catch damp, leaks and neglected maintenance while the repair is still cheap.

PDF reports

A clean, professional PDF for you, your tenant and your deposit scheme. Tap any photo in it to see it full size.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay £30 + VAT for each report: a check-in, a check-out or an inspection.

Do my tenants need to download an app?

No. They open a secure link in their phone’s browser to check and sign the report.

Can I use it on more than one property?

Yes. Use it on as many properties as you like. Each report is paid for separately.

Does this replace professional advice?

No. DIY Inventory creates dated records of a property’s condition. It is not legal advice. For a dispute or a specific question, speak to a qualified professional.
