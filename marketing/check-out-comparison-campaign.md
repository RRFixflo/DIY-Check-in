# Campaign kit: check-in vs check-out, compared by AI

**The idea in one line:** *"Stop comparing two reports page by page. Our AI compares every check-out photo with the check-in and lists what changed. £30."*

Why it wins:
- **The pain is real.** Comparing a 40-page check-in with a 40-page check-out takes an hour or more, and things get missed.
- **It's the evidence deposit disputes turn on.** You get the changes, how serious each one is, and the check-in photo beside today's.
- **It's fair to both sides.** Wear and tear is shown as wear and tear, not damage. Tenants trust it, and landlords only raise what will stand up.
- **It makes the check-in worth doing with us.** The comparison only works when the check-in was done in DIY Inventory, so every check-in is a future check-out.

## 2. Rules for the wording (keep it true)
- **Say:** "compares every photo", "lists what changed", "possible damage", "looks like wear and tear".
- **Never say:**
  - that the AI decides who pays or what to deduct;
  - "guaranteed", "100% accurate", or "accepted by deposit schemes";
  - anything about checking reports made elsewhere. It only compares against a DIY Inventory check-in.
- **No VAT anywhere.** £30 per report.
