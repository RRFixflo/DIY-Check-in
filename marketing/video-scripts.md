# Short video scripts — TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels

Vertical, 30–60 seconds, filmed on a phone. You don't need to be on camera — screen-record the app and talk over it,
or show hands doing a real walk-round. Post the same clip to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Keep the honest rules: free to start, £29.99, no subscription, no VAT; "made to be used as evidence" (never "wins
disputes"); clerk prices "typical", no named company. Put **diyinventory.co.uk** on screen at the end and in the caption.

**Hook (0–3s):** "An inventory clerk wants £150 for this. I'll do it myself in ten minutes — watch."
**Body (3–45s):** screen-record the app.
- "Open the link on your phone — nothing to install."
- "It walks you round room by room. Here's the kitchen — it tells me exactly what to photograph next."
- "Every photo gets the date and time burned *into* the picture — that's what makes it count as evidence."
- "Meters, keys, the front of the property — all in."
- "Sign at the end, and it builds the PDF right here on the phone."
**Close (45–55s):** "Free to start, £29.99 to download. No subscription, no VAT. It's at diyinventory.co.uk."

## Posting tips
- Hook in the first 2 seconds or they scroll. Lead with the number or the pain, not the brand.
- Text on screen (most watch muted). Caption the key lines.
- Post 2–3 a week; repost the ones that get views to the other platforms.
- End card: logo + diyinventory.co.uk + "free to start".
- Pin a comment with the link.
