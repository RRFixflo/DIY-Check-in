Breach
Point

Two squads, one house, one device. On attack you drone it out, blow a wall open and plant the defuser. On defence you reinforce, set traps and hold the room. Sides swap after every round.

Operator

Armory Your loadout is used on both sides

Bots

Graphics High adds ambient occlusion and bloom. Pick Medium if it stutters.

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Click Deploy to lock the mouse; press Esc to pause.

On attack

  1. PREP30s drone. Find the device room and click enemies to mark them.
  2. BREACHPale drywall breaks under fire and charges. Barricades fall to melee. Steel needs a thermal charge.
  3. WINEliminate all five, or hold F on the hazard square to plant the defuser and protect it for 35s.

On defence

  1. PREP35s setup. Aim at drywall and press T to turn it to steel. Place your gadget with G.
  2. HOLDAttackers path in and breach. Shoot through drywall to catch them early.
  3. WINEliminate them, run out the clock, or hold F next to a planted defuser for 6s to disable it.
