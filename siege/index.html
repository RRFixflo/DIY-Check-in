Breach
Point

Five defenders are dug in around a device somewhere in the house. Drone the building to find them, open a wall, and either take them all out or plant the defuser and hold it.

Operator

Defenders

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Click Deploy to lock the mouse; press Esc to pause.

How a round plays

  1. 30sPrep. You drive a drone. Find the device room and click on defenders to mark them. Marks last 20 seconds into the assault.
  2. 2:30Assault. Soft walls (pale panels) break under fire and charges. Barricades fall to a few hits. Grey steel walls need a thermal charge.
  3. WINEliminate all five, or stand on the hazard square and hold F to plant the defuser, then keep defenders off it for 35 seconds.
